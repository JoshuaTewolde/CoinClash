# CoinClash demo script (≈ 3:30)

Narration on the left, what's on screen on the right. Clips marked **[you]** are filmed on a real
phone; everything else is a Playwright clip of two phones side by side (Android left, iPhone
right) from `demo/` (file names in brackets). Speak at a relaxed pace, about 140 words a minute.

---

### 1. Hook (0:00–0:15)

| Say                                                                                                                                                                                                    | Show                                                                                       |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Every group chat has that one argument that only gets settled with a game. CoinClash lets you settle it properly: pick a game, put some sats on it, and the winner gets paid the second the game ends. | Title card, then **[01-home]**: Home on both phones, wallet balance, friends with records. |

### 2. Sign up with a passkey (0:15–0:40)

| Say                                                                                                                                                                                                  | Show                                                     |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------- |
| Getting started takes about ten seconds. There's no seed phrase and no password. You pick a nickname, touch your fingerprint, and the Breez SDK derives a Bitcoin wallet straight from your passkey. | **[you]** Create account → fingerprint prompt → Home.    |
| Your wallet lives on your device, it syncs with your passkey, and you even get a few sats to start with.                                                                                             | **[you]** Wallet card filling in with the welcome bonus. |

### 3. Challenging a friend (0:40–1:10)

| Say                                                                                                                                                                                 | Show                                                                                                                                    |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| To start a match you choose a game and a stake. Stakes are always in sats, with the dollar value right next to them so you know what you're risking.                                | **[02-new-challenge]** Pick Connect 4, type a stake, "≈ $" updates.                                                                     |
| If they're not on CoinClash yet, you share a link, a QR code, or a six-digit code they can type in.                                                                                 | **[03-invite-join]** Invite screen with QR and code; the iPhone types the code and joins.                                               |
| Once you've played someone, they show up as a friend, and you can challenge them directly. It lands on their phone instantly, as a push notification or right on their home screen. | **[04-direct-challenge]** Android challenges, iPhone gets the toast and "Waiting on you". **[you]** Push notification on a lock screen. |

You can counter the stake until you agree.                                                                                    | **[05-negotiate]** Counter-offer, then accept.                                                                                          |

### 4. The money (1:10–1:40)

| Say                                                                                                                                                                                          | Show                                                                                                                  |
| -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Before anyone moves, both stakes are paid into escrow. Each player gets their own Spark invoice that only their wallet can pay, so the payment is instant, and it settles in under a second. | **[06-pay-stake]** Both tap Pay; "Secured by CoinClash · powered by Breez"; the board appears on both phones at once. |
| And you don't have to take our word for it. The escrow page shows, live, what the escrow holds against what it owes to every match in play.                                                  | **[14-escrow]** Held vs Owed, "✓ Fully covered", recent payouts.                                                      |

### 5. The games (1:40–2:45)

| Say                                                                                                                                                                                         | Show                                                                                                                         |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| There are five games right now, and everything is played turn by turn in real time. When I drop a disc on one phone, it lands on the other.                                                 | **[07-connect4]** Alternating drops, synced on both phones.                                                                  |
| Rock paper scissors is simultaneous: both picks stay hidden until you've both chosen, and then they clash.                                                                                  | **[08-rps]** Both pick, the reveal, "Rock breaks scissors".                                                                  |
| Trivia is a race to the right answer, five questions with sudden death if you're tied.                                                                                                      | **[09-trivia]** Both answer, the reveal with scores.                                                                         |
| And yes, there's pool. Turn your phone sideways for the full table, drag to aim, pull the cue down for power and let go. | Every game runs on the server, so nobody can cheat by editing their app, and if someone stalls out, the clock ends it and the pot goes to the player who showed up.                         | **[11-tictactoe]** A quick game, the turn timer counting down.                                                               |
